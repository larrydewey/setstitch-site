SetStitch privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-05. Contact: setstitch.app@gmail.com.

SetStitch is a nutrition, training and progress tracker. It is built to keep your data on your phone. This policy explains what stays there and what leaves it.

On your phone only

Your food diary, workouts, body measurements, progress photos, steps, sleep, cycle and intimacy logs, and settings are stored on your device. We (the SetStitch developer) never receive them.

Data from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) is read and written only on your device. It is never uploaded to our server and never shared with third parties.

When something leaves your phone

Feature What is sent To whom
Food search and barcode scans the search words or barcode Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database, Health Canada
Nearby stores (optional) your approximate location OpenStreetMap's Overpass service, to list nearby shops
Recipe import (optional) the link you paste the website the link points to

Cloud backup (optional) your data, end-to-end encrypted with your passphrase the storage you choose (Google Drive, WebDAV or S3). Neither we nor the provider can read it.
Friends & challenges (optional, off by default) see below SetStitch server, hosted by Supabase

Friends & challenges

This feature is off until you turn it on and sign in. If you use it, the SetStitch server stores:

  • Account: your email address and sign-in provider (Google or email link), handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Profile: handle, display name, optional photo, bio, goal tags and visibility setting.
  • Friends and blocks: who you have added or blocked.
  • Posts: meals, recipes, workouts, templates or programs you choose to post, with small stats (for example, workout minutes).
  • Challenges: for each challenge you join, one number per day for that challenge's measure (for example, your step total). Nothing else from your health log is uploaded.
  • Messages: chat messages you send, including edits.
  • Reports you file about other users' content.

Who can see it: your profile and posts are visible according to your visibility setting (only me, friends or everyone; the default is friends). Challenge members see each other's challenge numbers. Chat messages are visible only to that chat's members. Blocked users cannot see your profile, posts or messages.

We use this data only to run these features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or share it with third parties. Supabase (our hosting provider) processes it on our behalf.

Deleting your data

  • Online data: in the app, open More › Friends & challenges › your profile › Edit › Delete account. This removes your account and everything listed above from the server immediately. You can also email setstitch.app@gmail.com from the account's address, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
  • Export first: Edit profile › Export my online data gives you a copy.
  • Data on your phone: remove it with Settings › Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

SetStitch is not for children under 13. Friends & challenges asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Server access is restricted per user by row-level security. Backups are end-to-end encrypted.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes.

Delete your SetStitch account

SetStitch keeps your diary, workouts and health data on your phone. An account exists only if you used Friends & challenges.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open More › Friends & challenges.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right), then Edit (pencil).
  3. Optional: Export my online data to keep a copy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Send an email from the address you signed in with to setstitch.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my SetStitch account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your profile and photo, friends and blocks, posts, challenge numbers, challenges you created (for all members), chat messages you sent, and reports you filed. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires it.

Data on your phone is not affected. Remove it in the app with Settings › Delete all data, or by uninstalling SetStitch.

Contact: setstitch.app@gmail.com
